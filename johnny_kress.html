Background

Johnny Kress grew up on the North Shore of O‘ahu and picked up a kendama in October 2013 as a young kid in Hawai‘i. He linked up with Sweets Kendamas as a sponsored shredder while still a teenager and was officially named to the Sweets Team (the brand’s pro roster) in 2019. According to Sweets’ product write-ups, the team has watched him “progress in kendama and grow as a person since meeting him as a young shredder in Hawai‘i.”

His competitive résumé spans nearly a decade of major events — from Kendama World Cup appearances in Japan to North American open contests — with multiple podium finishes online and in-person. He is best known publicly under the handles @jkress808 (TikTok) and @j.kress (Instagram), where he posts trick edits, contest runs, and lifestyle clips.
